How to assess the results

Proofs, measurements, and scope

Exact savings need exact structure

Core and block theorems give sufficient tractable regimes. They do not establish that every subset-sum input has a cheap representation.

Measured gains include acquisition costs

Geometric reuse improves work-capped completions from 453 to 469 on 576 fresh constructed targets. Later refinements mainly save construction; the reported comparisons remain separate.

Prediction carries a different guarantee

Bucket mass and purity are population statements for a fixed solver and budget. The new demonstration uses synthetic costs; a fresh paid subset-sum evaluation remains open.
