Equations behind the curves

\[N=2^n=C+R,\qquad R=N-C.\]
\[H=\lambda C,\quad \lambda\ge1,\qquad T=H+R=N+(\lambda-1)C\ge N.\]

Here \(n\) counts binary choices; \(N\) counts candidate assignments; \(C\) counts candidates certified collectively; \(R\) counts candidates searched explicitly; \(\lambda\) is work per covered candidate in the plotted schedule; \(H\) is certification work; and \(T\) is total work. Net saving is \(S=N-T=C-H=(1-\lambda)C\le0\) in this restricted model.
